## 1. Executive Overview & Audit Instructions

### Purpose of this Pack
This document exposes every raw string, phrase, pattern, dialogue line, story paragraph, and audio cue currently seeded in the application codebase for Levels 1 through 3. It strips away technical abstractions so that editors, language specialists, Islamic pedagogy advisors, and product owners can inspect the material exactly as a child experiences it.

### Review Instructions for Human Auditors
For each table:
- **Product Owner Decision**: Check `[ ] Keep`, `[ ] Revise`, or `[ ] Remove`.
- **Notes**: Annotate with specific corrections (e.g., tone adjustments, phonological simplifications, regional dialect considerations, cultural alignment).

### 5.1 Dialogue 1: Polite Dining Request (`func_polite_request_food`)
- **Level**: 3 | **Communicative Intent**: `requesting` | **World**: `world_food`

- **Islamic / Character Connection**: Etiquette of asking courteously, saying *Bismillah* before drinking, and *Alhamdulillah* after drinking.
